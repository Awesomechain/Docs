# The Feed

**English** · [中文](../zh/feed.md)

The home page is a live feed of everything happening on Awesome.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | everything below, newest first |
| **Launches** | new coins and graduations, with $牛逼 pinned on top |
| **Posts** | posts from the community |
| **X** | posts from the accounts that move BNB Chain: BNB Chain, Binance, PancakeSwap, four.meme, flap.sh, the 牛逼 community and more |
| **Trades** | big buys of Awesome coins, and $牛逼 buys and sells |

- X posts that mention a `$TICKER` or a contract address known to Awesome link straight to that coin.
- Posts in another language can be translated in place (English ↔ 中文).

## Posting

Connect a wallet and sign a message (free, no transaction) to post. Posts are plain text, up to 280 characters, and
can tag a coin. Links are limited to x.com, t.me, niúbī.xyz and bscscan.com. Limits: one post every 30 seconds and 50
a day per wallet.
