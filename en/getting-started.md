# Getting started

**English** · [中文](../zh/getting-started.md)

## 1. Open the site

Go to **[niúbī.xyz](https://xn--nib-9na0w.xyz)** (in some browsers and wallets the address shows as
`xn--nib-9na0w.xyz`: it is the same site). The language switch in the header flips between English and 中文.

## 2. Connect a wallet

Click **Connect**.

- **EVM chains** (BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, HyperEVM, Monad, X Layer, Elysium, Robinhood Chain, Arc): any injected
  wallet (MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Binance Wallet, Rabby, Trust…) or WalletConnect.
- **Solana**: any Solana wallet that supports the Wallet Standard (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, OKX…).

You only need gas in the chain's native coin: BNB on BNB Chain, ETH on Base, Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain, HYPE on
HyperEVM and Elysium, MON on Monad, OKB on X Layer, USDC on Arc, SOL on Solana.

## 3. Find a coin

- **Home (the Feed)** shows launches, graduations and big buys as they happen. See [The Feed](feed.md).
- **Coins** lists every coin on every chain, sorted by new, trending, market cap, closest to graduating or most
  burned. The chain filter picks which chains you see.
- **Search** (top bar) finds a coin by name, ticker or contract address.

A coin page shows the chart, the progress to graduation, trades, holders, the coin's launch type and where its fees
went.

## 4. Buy and sell

On the coin page, enter an amount in the trade box and press **Buy** or **Sell**. Your wallet asks you to confirm
the transaction. Slippage is set in the trade box (default 1%). See [Trading](trading.md) for how prices move.

## 5. Launch your own

Press **Launch** in the header. It takes about a minute: see [Launching a coin](launching.md).

## Stay safe

- The only official site is **niúbī.xyz** (`xn--nib-9na0w.xyz`). Check the address before you connect.
- Awesome never asks for your seed phrase or private key.
- Official contract addresses are on the [Chains](chains.md) page and in the
  [Contracts](https://github.com/Awesomechain/Contracts) repo.
- Anyone can launch a coin. A coin being on Awesome doesn't mean Awesome endorses it.
