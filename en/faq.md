# FAQ

**English** · [中文](../zh/faq.md)

**What does 牛逼 mean?**
"Awesome" in Chinese (niúbī). 牛 is the bull.

**How much does it cost to launch?**
Only gas, plus your first buy if you make one.

**Can a creator pull the liquidity?**
No. On the EVM chains the whole supply is locked in the pool forever, and no function removes liquidity.

**What happens at graduation?**
The coin keeps trading in the same pool, with the same fee. Nothing migrates.

**Why did my buy right after launch cost so much?**
The first 10 seconds are the sniper window: a 50% fee and at most 1% of supply per buy.

**Where do the fees go?**
60% to the coin's launch type, 10% to the platform, 10% to 牛逼 staking and 20% to buying back and burning $牛逼.
See [Fees](fees.md).

**Can I change my coin's launch type later?**
No. It is fixed at launch, so holders can trust it.

**How do I claim my creator fees?**
Profile → Claim. See [Claiming](claiming.md).

**I launched on the wrong chain. Can I move the coin?**
No, a coin lives on the chain it was launched on.

**Is there an API?**
Yes, plus an SDK, verified contracts and agent skills. See [Developers](developers.md).
