## What drives $牛逼

- **Buyback and burn from every chain.** 20% of every trading fee on Awesome, on every chain, buys back $牛逼 and
  burns it.
- **Bull Run coins.** Coins launched as [Bull Run](launch-types.md#bull-run) spend their whole creator share buying
  $牛逼 and sending it to the burn address.
- **Staking.** 10% of every trading fee is set aside for 牛逼 staking.

Burned $牛逼 goes to `0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD`, where no one can ever move it. The $牛逼 page shows
the total burned, the circulating supply, the price, the pools and every trade.

## On Solana

$牛逼 can also live on Solana. When it does, its page gets a **BNB Chain / Solana** switch, and the footer of the site
shows both contract addresses. Only trust the addresses shown on niúbī.xyz.
